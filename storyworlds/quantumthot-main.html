Interactive Narrative

Storyworlds as Interpretability Tools

Well-designed interactive narratives encode compressed, interpretable representations of complex systems. This research program tests whether training AI on narrative-structured data produces more interpretable internal representations.

21 Years of Research

Long-term collaboration with Chris Crawford and the interactive storytelling community. Storyworlds aren't just entertainment — they're computational structures that force coherent representations of agents, goals, and causality.

Core Hypothesis

If we train models on richly-structured narrative data and extract sparse autoencoders, do we get features that map more cleanly to human-understandable concepts than models trained on unstructured text? That's what this research will find out.
