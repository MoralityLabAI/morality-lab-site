    03 · Evaluation stages

    From talk to consequential action.

    Prose-only, malformed, abstained, and nonexecuted responses stay in the denominator. The evaluator does not change with the character.

    01

    Talking eval

    Can it commit after explaining?

    Dialogue presents the dilemma, but only the declared final action counts.

    02

    Storyworld eval

    Does its choice change the world?

    Actions alter public state, character trust, access, and later options.

    03

    Multi-agent eval

    Can its policy survive a council?

    Agents negotiate through attributable proposals, objections, and votes.

    The research boundary

    Public architecture, not a window into private thought.

    The village visualizes selected component IDs, registered transitions, receipts, and final actions. Its constitutions are exploratory normative frames pending review—not theological authorities, moral rankings, or model-backed findings.
